
With the misty night's cold stabbing at their necks, the three newly found companions have finally found their destination at Westpier street. Coming out of the building line, they smelled the [[Jeggare River]] before ever seeing it - a reek of brine assaulted their noses even from across the road. On the other side of the quiet street stood an old fishery - a creaking, decrepit building of rotting wood, with boarded windows and doors falling on their hinges. The entire fishery perched atop a a steep embankment coming out of the river - it's a small miracle the entire thing hasn't collapsed and sunk beneath the waves years ago.  

"We should be stealthy about this. We don't know what's expecting us inside." said [[Barton]], and went ahead to scout. Crossing the road with his gun out, he ran to the barred windows to take a peak between the bars. The room he looked into seemed dark at first, but on a second glance he noticed small characters moving about the room, stirring what looked like a massive cooking pot. Stepping to the right to check the next room which contained the entrance door, he made a miscalculation, and his steps were heard from inside, triggering a baleful sound from some animal - something horrendous between a donkey's bray and a powerful wheeze. Worried that he'll soon be noticed, Barton was getting ready to leave, when he notice an interesting feature - a slippery boardwalk clinging to the side of the fishery and leading behind it, to an old sailing ship.  At this point, the unknown beast's commotion seemed to have alerted a man - Barton could not hear exactly who he was - and so he quickly made his way back to his friends hiding across the street. "I may have just given our position away" Barton frowned, "but I also found a back entrance for us. We just have to quickly go through the walkway and we can catch Gaedren by surprise - it's even probable he is on that ship."  

The three ran over to the boardwalk, the unsettling braying still filling the quiet night. Barton was the first to cross. Though the planks were slippery of brine water and the pilings that held them barnacle-encrusted and worn, Barton proved agile on his feet, hopping the planks like a hare and soon disappearing behind view. [[Zim]] moved next, and though he was more careful with his steps, the young Varisian also crossed the boardwalk with ease. Finally, it was [[Kebub Urag|Kebub's]] time to go. He realized something was wrong from the very first steps he took. The planks creaked and groaned under his [[Orc|Orcish]] muscle and heavy armor. Still, he could make it through if he was careful enough, he thought. Slowly, he took one more step and one more step, one after the other.  

The walkway crashed around him with a deafening sound as he plunged into the cold murky water.
