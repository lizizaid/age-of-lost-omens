Children of [[Elf|elves]] and other ancestries, **Half Elves** combine the strengths of both their parental heritages, though they might fail to fit in either. In Elven, they are known as **Aiuvarins**, a word that describes leaves that fall too quickly to the ground.  

### Description
Half elves are born when an elf and a member of some other ancestry rear a child, or, more commonly, from half-elf parents or some elvish inheritance in the family. The most common half elves found originate from unions between [[Human|humans]] and elves, although half elves with a [[Dwarf|dwarven]] or [[Orc|orcish]] ancestry are not unheard of.  

Half elves tend to be a little taller than the average specimen of their non-elven ancestry, and they keep the elven lean builds and elongated ears. They are also blessed with some of their elven's parent longevity, and often live for around 150 years. This causes some of them to fear friendship and romance with humans, knowing that they'll likely outlive their companions.  

### Society
Because most Aiuvarins do not fit completely into the cultures of unmixed ancestries, they tend to adapt to the society in which they feel most comfortable. This versatility makes them excellent and indispensable survivors as they work hard to seek acceptance from those around them.  

Among humans, for instance, they are appreciated for their elven beauty, height, and grace, while the elves appreciate in them the same traits they value in humans: spontaneity, freedom, and excitement. However, elves often underestimate half-elves, while non-elves often disregard their non-elven ancestry.  

Their mixed ancestry can create a conflict within them that leads them to performative occupations such as art and entertainment, but also keeps them from feeling like they are part of their parents' communities or forming long-lasting bonds to them. For half elves with a shorter-lived parent, their longer lifespan also introduces fear into their attempts to form relationships with individuals whom they know they will probably outlive.
