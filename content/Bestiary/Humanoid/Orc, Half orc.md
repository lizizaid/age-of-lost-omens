Descendant of relationships between [[Orc|orcs]] and other humanoids, **Half Orcs** combine the raw strength of their orcish ancestors with the graces of smaller folk. They are children of both worlds and of neither, considered brutish and barbaric by the civilized people, and weaklings in orc terms - but nevertheless, a useful bridge for both peoples. In Orcish they are known as **Drommar**, a word referring to those drummers whose music sends the horde marching into war.  

### Description
Half orcs are most commonly born out of unions between orcs and [[Human|humans]], though half-orcs-half-[[Elf|elves]] or [[Dwarf|dwarves]] also exist. They vary widely in appearance, ranging from more orc-like features such as greenish skin and protruding lower tusks to nearly human with little more to distinguish them as half orcs than prominent noses, jawlines, and brows. Some offspring of orcs and humans have dominant human traits that make them appear completely human.  

Regardless of facial appearance, virtually every dromaar has a stature far larger than that of the average human. Their hair most often is dark, favoring shades of brown, gray, or even dark red, although black is the most common. Their eyes are often small and beady, while their ears are almost always pointed. Their average lifespan is 70 years.  

### Society
Most half orc lineages can trace their history to unions between orc tribes and other tribal civilizations, though some are the product of violence and perversion. When encountered in civilized lands, drommars are often the subjects of prejudice and even violence, as their bestial facial features inspire terror and revulsion - especially in areas subject to frequent orc assaults. Those who live instead in orc societies generally find that their more fragile forms compared to their relatives can also be a boon, and many orc tribes utilize their drommars as diplomats, scouts and strategists.
