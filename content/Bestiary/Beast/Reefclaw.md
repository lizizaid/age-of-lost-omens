**Reefclaws** are vicious, aquatic predators that are commonly found throughout the coasts and rivers of the [[The World of Golarion#The Inner Sea|Inner Sea region]]. They possess greater intelligence than most animals, but mainly rely on their strength, instinct, and potent venom to survive.  

### Description
Reefclaws resemble a cross between a spiny crustacean, such as a shrimp or a lobster, and an eel. An adult reefclaw typically stretches between 1 and 1.2 meters in length.  

Reefclaws usually dwell in cool or warm coasts and rivers, where they feed on fish, crustaceans, dolphins, and land-dwelling creatures when these come close enough to be ambushed. Reefclaws inject a powerful venom through their claws, which can quickly pacify animals and even humans.  

### Ecology
Reefclaws are usually solitary hunters, but small swarms of female reefclaws have been known to gather around a single male for mating purposes or together for the communal raising of their broods. In the latter case, the females will perform widespread hunts in order to bring back a large enough bounty to feed their young. Such hunting parties are extremely dangerous—they've been known to tip over small fishing boats and attack those who fall overboard.  

Once a reefclaw has decided upon a course of action, it follows through even if doing so spells its own end. More than one reefclaw survivor has said that the creature released its bone-crushing grasp only after the brains were leaking from its broken skull, and even then, the reefclaw was able to perform a terrible last slash as part of its dying breath. During mating season, female reefclaws are often a little more pragmatic and release their prey before endangering themselves and their offspring.   

### Society
Though reefclaws can't speak, they are intelligent enough to understand the local language of humanoids near their hunting grounds. The creatures sometimes listen to conversations, either to gain intelligence on the best place to ambush aquatic or land-dwelling prey, or merely for entertainment value—reefclaws are particularly fond of listening to people with high-pitched voices.  

Despite their intelligence and the accompanying moral quandaries, reefclaws frequently find their way onto the dinner plates of land-dwelling hunters. According to those who have a taste for reefclaw flesh, the meat is either delectably sweet (for reefclaws raised in colder waters) or slightly tangy (in the case of warm-water reefclaws). Most people who know of reefclaws' intellect find the act of eating them distasteful, but this does not dissuade unscrupulous nobles in coastal regions, for whom reefclaw meat is a delicacy well worth the expense. Likewise, fishers whose focus is on the bottom line of their ledgers are more than willing to hunt the dangerous creatures—or, even better, hire out the task to naive adventurers.
