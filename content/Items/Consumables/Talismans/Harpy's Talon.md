### Info
- **Level:** 2
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Talisman
- **Cost**: 13 GP  

### Description
This sharp metal claw is used to inflict additional pain upon the bearer's enemies.  

### Usage
**Usage** Affixed to a slashing weapon; **Duration** 1 minute; For the duration, the weapon deals persistent bleed damage equal to the number of weapon damage dice. If the strike was a critical success, the persistent bleed damage increases to twice the number of weapon damage dice.
