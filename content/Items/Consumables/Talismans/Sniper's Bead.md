- **Cost**: 
	- 46 GP

	- 4,600 GP (Major)

### Description
This plain wooden bead dangles from a string attached to the stock of the affixed weapon. When you activate the bead, its magic greatly lessens the effect of distance on your triggering attack.  

### Usage
**Usage** affixed to a two-handed firearm or crossbow; **Requirements** You're a master with the affixed weapon; **Duration** 1 minute; For the duration, you take no range increment penalty on your attack, as long as the target is within the affixed weapon's first 2 range increments.  

(Greater) The effect is for the affixed weapon's first 4 range increments.  
(Major) The effect is all the way out for the affixed weapon's 6th range increment.
