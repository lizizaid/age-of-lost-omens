### Info
- **Level:** 3
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Talisman
- **Cost**: 18 GP

### Description
This stone, shapes as a cabochon, is a small chunk of amber with a bit of feather or flying insect caught within it. Activating it makes the feet fleet and dexterous.  

### Usage
**Requirements** You are trained in Acrobatics; **Usage** affixed to armor; **Duration** 1 minute; For the duration, you ignore the effects of any Difficult Terrain you move through, and treat Greater Difficult Terrain as Difficult Terrain.
