### Info
- **Level:** 
	- 4
	- 8 (Greater)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Potion
- **Cost**:

	- 85 GP (Greater)

### Description
This potion is colorless and oddly lightweight.  

### Usage
**Activate** > (manipulate); You drink the potion, gaining the effects of a 2nd-rank [Invisibility Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1577&Redirected=1).  
(Greater) You gain the effects of a 4th-level invisibility spell.
