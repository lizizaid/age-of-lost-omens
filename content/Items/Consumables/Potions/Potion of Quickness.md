### Info
- **Level:** 8
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Potion
- **Cost**: 90 GP

### Description
The particles of this silvery potion seems to always move and bubble around.  

### Usage
**Activate** > (manipulate); You drink the potion, becoming Quickened for 1 minute. You can use the extra action only to Stride, Strike, or as part of Casting a Cantrip.
