### Info

	- 6 (Lesser)
	- 10 (Moderate)
	- 14 (Greater)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Potion
- **Cost**:
	- 45 GP (Lesser)
	- 180 GP (Moderate)
	- 850 GP (Greater)

### Description
This thick and sluggish potion grants the body increased resistance against a certain damage type.  

### Usage
**Activate** > (manipulate); You gain resistance against a single damage type for 1 hour. Each potion is created to defend against a certain damage type, such as a potion of fire resistance, potion of slashing resistance, etc.  

(Lesser) You gain resistance 5 to the appropriate damage type.  
(Moderate) You gain resistance 10 to the appropriate damage type.  
(Greater) You gain resistance 15 to the appropriate damage type.
