### Info
- **Level:** 5
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Magic Weapon (Dagger)
- **Cost**: 115 gp

### Description
This crystalline dagger is enchanted with protective spells, a favorite among spellcasters.  

### Usage
This is a [[Weapon Rune, Potency|+1 Potency]] [[Spellcasting Rune, Potency|+1 Spellcasting]] dagger. As long as it is invested, it grants the wielder a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws against spells of the Arcane tradition.
