### Info
- **Level:** 5
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Magic Weapon (Morningstar)
- **Cost**: 165 gp

### Description
This morningstar hits hard on major blows, shaking bones and nerves.  

### Usage
This [[Weapon Rune, Potency|+1]] [[Weapon Rune, Striking|Striking]] morningstar grants the following reaction:  
<- **Trigger** You critically hit a creature with Abrupt Force; The creature must succeed at a DC 20 fortitude save or become Stunned 1 (Stunned 2 on a critical failure).
