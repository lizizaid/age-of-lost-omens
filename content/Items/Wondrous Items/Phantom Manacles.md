### Info
- **Level:** 3
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Type**: Wondrous Item
- **Cost**: 90 GP

### Description
These enchanted manacles can operate on their own, flying to arrest escaping criminals at a distance.  

### Usage
These are magical average quality manacles. They have the following ability:  
**Activate - Catch them!** >> (Envision, Interact); **Frequency** Once per day; **Effect** you let loose of the manacles, who Fly towards an enemy within 60 feet of you and attempt to lock themselves over it's wrists. The creature must attempt a DC 23 Reflex saving throw.  

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The manacles are loosely placed around the creature's wrists. Removing the manacles requires a single action.  
**Failure** The manacles clamp around the creature's wrists. The creature is Off-Guard and Clumsy 1 until the manacles are removed as a 3-action activity.  
**Critical Failure** The manacles are clamped around the creature's wrists and locked tight. The creature is flat-footed and clumsy 1 until the manacles are removed (Escape DC 23, simple lock).
