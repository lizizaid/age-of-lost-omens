### Info
- **Level:** 
	- 1 (Lesser)
	- 5 (Moderate)
	- 10 (Greater)
	- 16 (Major)
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Elixir
- **Cost**: 
	- 4 GP (Lesser)
	- 27 GP (Moderate)
	- 200 GP (Greater)
	- 2,000 GP (Major)

### Description
After you drink this elixir, your pupils expand, and you notice subtle visual details.  

### Usage
**Duration** 1 hour; You gain a bonus to Perception checks that's greater when attempting to find secret doors and traps.  

(Lesser) The bonus is +1, or +2 to find secret doors and traps.  
(Moderate) The bonus is +2, or +3 to find secret doors and traps.  
(Greater) The bonus is +3, or +4 to find secret doors and traps.  
(Major) The bonus is +4, or +5 to find secret doors and traps. Each time you pass within 10 feet of a secret door or trap, the GM automatically rolls a secret check for you to find it.
