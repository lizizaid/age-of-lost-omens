### Description
Alchemist's fire is a combination of volatile liquids that ignite when exposed to air.  

### Usage
**Activate** > (Strike) The flask deals the listed fire damage, persistent fire damage, and splash damage. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.  

(Lesser) The bomb deals 1d8 fire damage, 1 persistent fire damage, and 1 fire splash damage.  
(Moderate) You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 2d8 fire damage, 2 persistent fire damage, and 2 fire splash damage.  
(Greater) You gain a +2 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 3d8 fire damage, 3 persistent fire damage, and 3 fire splash damage.  
(Major) You gain a +3 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 4d8 fire damage, 4 persistent fire damage, and 4 fire splash damage.  
(Supreme) You gain a +4 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 5d8 fire damage, 5 persistent fire damage, and 5 fire splash damage.
