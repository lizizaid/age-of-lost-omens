### Description
This flask is filled with corrosive alchemical acid.  

### Usage
**Activate** > (Strike) The flask deals 1 acid damage, the listed persistent acid damage, and listed acid splash damage. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.  

(Lesser) The bomb deals 1d6 persistent acid damage and 1 acid splash damage.  
(Moderate) You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 2d6 persistent acid damage and 2 acid splash damage.  
(Greater) You gain a +2 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 3d6 persistent acid damage and 3 acid splash damage.  
(Major) You gain a +3 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 4d6 persistent acid damage and 4 acid splash damage.  
(Supreme) You gain a +4 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 5d6 persistent acid damage and 5 acid splash damage.
