### Description
This brilliant blazing rune increases the effectiveness of the spellcaster's magical healing.  

### Usage
Whenever you cast a spell from a ranked spell slot that has the Healing trait and restores health points, you restore an additional 1d8 health points.  

(Greater) You restore an additional 2d8 health points.  
(Major) You restore an additional 3d8 health points.    
(True) You restore an additional 4d8 health points.
