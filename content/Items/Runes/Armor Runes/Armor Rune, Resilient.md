### Description
This rune imbues armor with additional protective magic, increasing the reflexes, fortitude, and will power of the wearer.  

### Usage
You gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws. ou can upgrade the _resilient rune_ already etched on a suit of armor using the normal process for upgrading runes.  

(Greater) The armor grants a +2 item bonus to saving throws.
(Major) The armor grants a +3 item bonus to saving throws.  
(Supreme) The armor grants a +4 item bonus to saving throws.
