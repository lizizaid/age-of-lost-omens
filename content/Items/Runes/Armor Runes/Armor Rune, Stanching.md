### Description
These magical symbols close bloody wounds.  

### Usage
Armor with this rune reduces the DC of the flat check to end persistent bleed damage from 15 to 12 (7 with particularly effective assistance).  

(Greater) The DC of the flat check is reduced from 15 to 10 (5 with particularly effective assistance).  
(Major) The DC of the flat check is reduced from 15 to 8 (3 with particularly effective assistance).  
(True) The DC of the flat check is reduced from 15 to 5 (particularly effective assistance automatically removes the persistent bleed damage).
