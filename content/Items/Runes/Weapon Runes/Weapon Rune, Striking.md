### Description
This rune enchants the weapon with destructive power, increasing it's damage.  

### Usage
A striking rune increasing the weapon damage dice it deals to two instead of one. For instance, a _+1 striking dagger_ would deal 2d4 damage instead of 1d4 damage.  
You can upgrade the _striking rune_ already etched on a weapon to a stronger version, increasing the values of the existing rune to those of the new rune. The Price of the upgrade is the difference between the two runes' Prices.  

(Greater) The weapon deals three weapon damage dice.  
(Major) The weapon deals four weapon damage dice.  
(Supreme) The weapon deals five weapon damage dice.
