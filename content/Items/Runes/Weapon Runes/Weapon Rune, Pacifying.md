	- 150 GP

	- 25,000 GP (Major)

### Description
This rune turns weapons into instruments of peacemaking.  

### Usage
You gain the following reaction:  
**Activate** <- (concentrate, mental); **Trigger** You damage a creature with the etched weapon; **Effect** The damaged creature must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be pacified. A pacified creature takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls on any attacks that aren't nonlethal for 1 minute, and the creature also experiences a clear psychic warning that they should stop making attacks that could kill.  

(Greater) The DC increases to 30.  
(Major) The DC increases to 36.
