### Info
- **Level:** 9
- **Rarity**: Common
- **Type**: Weapon Rune
- **Cost**: 700 GP

### Description
This powerful enhancement increases the magnitude of the weapon's most deadly attacks.   

### Usage
When your attack roll with this weapon is a critical hit and gains the critical specialization effect, you gain an additional benefit depending on the weapon group.  

**[Axe](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=1)** You can damage a third creature, with the same restrictions.  
**[Bow](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=3)** The Athletics check to pull the missile free is DC 20.  
**[Brawling](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=4)** The target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its save.  
**[Club](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=5)** You can knock the target up to 15 feet away.  
**[Crossbow](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=17)** If the target of the critical hit is adjacent to a surface, it gets stuck to that surface by the missile, as the [bow](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=3) critical specialization.  
**[Dart](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=6)** The base persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6.  
**[Firearm](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=16)** The target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its save.  
**[Flail](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=7)** You move the target 5 feet. You can't move it away from you, but you can move it in another direction of your choice.  
**[Hammer](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=8)** You can also knock the target 5 feet away from you.  
**[Knife](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=9)** The target takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speed while it has the persistent bleed damage.  
**[Pick](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=10)** The extra damage from the critical specialization effect increases to 4 per weapon damage die.  
**[Polearm](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=11)** You can move the target up to 10 feet.  
**[Shield](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=12)** You can knock the target up to 10 feet away.  
**[Sling](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=13)** The target also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round if it fails the save.  
**[Spear](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=14)** The [clumsy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=3) condition lasts for 2 rounds.  
**[Sword](https://2e.aonprd.com/WeaponGroups.aspx?ID=15)** The target is off-guard until the end of your next turn.
