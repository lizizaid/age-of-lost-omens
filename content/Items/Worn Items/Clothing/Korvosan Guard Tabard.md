### Info
- **Level:** 5
- **Rarity**: Uncommon
- **Type**: Clothing
- **Cost**: 150 GP

### Description
This silvery tabard with bright red markings proudly presents the [[Korvosan Guard]] coat of arms. Only the most trusted officers get to wear this enchanted variant.  

### Usage
While in [[Korvosa]], the tabard grants a +1 item bonus to Demoralize checks, as well as a +1 item bonus to Will saving throws against Fear effects. In addition, the tabard grants you an aura of authority in the city, granting a +2 bonus to Make an Impression or Coerce when the target highly thinks of, or highly fears the Korvosan Guard.
