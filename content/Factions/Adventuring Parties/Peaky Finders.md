## Description
Recently formed, the Peaky Finders are a mercenary group of adventurers operating in Korvosa. Each has their reasons to defend the city, as well as dark secrets to keep.  

## History
The Peaky Finders have come together by chance - or, some might say, by fate - as each of the original members have been summoned by [[Zellara Esmeranda]] to take their revenge on [[Gaedren Lamm]] for some past wound the old miscreant has inflicted upon them. Since then, they have decided to stick together, believing that together their company can achieve more than alone - but also literally tied together by the invisible threads of fate.
