## Info
Orphans and street kids organized by a cruel thief to perform petty crimes
- **Type** criminal organization (gang)
- **Leader** Kester (Previously [[Gaedren Lamm]])
- **Location** [[Korvosa]]
- **Goals** Earn money through petty crime

## Description
This small street gang consists of crime lord Gaedren Lamm, a handful of his brutes and advisors, and about three dozen orphaned kids collected by Lamm from the streets. These children, whom he calls his "little lambs", are tough kids made rough as leather by life on the streets and by Gaedren's quick hand. They work for Lamm, cutting purses or pickpocketing bystanders by day, and cooking fish slew to sell to the homeless or run drugs by night. The urchins hate the old man, but fear him more than [[Asmodeus]] himself.   

## History
For years, Lamm has found delight in running his criminal operation using street urchins - they managed to gain him a steady income while not drawing to much attention from the authorities, and also satisfying Lamm's cruel need for control and torment. Most of his Lambs did not survive long in his care - those who did usually grew up to be hateful and dreary people just like their master.  

In 4709 AD, justice has finally caught up with old Lamm, when the members of the [[Peaky Finders]] busted his operations and put a stop to the scoundrel once and for all. They managed to convince the oldest boy in the remaining group, Kester, to leave the street ways behind and have the children do something meaningful with their lives. Kester has led his gang to try and find themselves at the Temple of [[Sarenrae]] - time will only tell what these kids will get up to next...
