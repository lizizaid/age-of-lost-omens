## Description
The Korvosan Guard serves their city first, their monarch second, and the church of [[Abadar]] third. It maintains order in the city, but also turns into a military operation whenever the city is threatened by external forces. Members of the guard are known for their signature red tabards.  

## History
Descendant from imperial troops that wrestled the region from the [[Varisia#Shoanti|Shoanti]], the Guard has a long and proud history of service to the city. Serving in the Guard is a high honor for Korvosa's militant and traditionalist citizens, and many noble houses groom their children to achieve high ranks in the chain of command.  

Today, the Korvosan Guard has about 900 soldiers, with the highest ranking member being the Field Marshal.  

## Dogma
The Guard's priority above all else is to defend the people of the city. To that end, they are supported by the church of [[Abadar]], and work closely with both the [[Sable Company]] and the Hellknight [[Order of the Nail]].
